Prayer Lock: Delete your account

App: Prayer Lock by Tetra Developers (Android and iPhone).

Option 1: delete it in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Prayer Lock.
  2. Tap the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  4. Tap Delete everything to confirm.

If the app says "please log out, sign in again, then delete", sign out, sign in again and repeat the steps.

Option 2: ask us by email

If you cannot open the app, email tetraheaddevelopers@gmail.com from the email address you used to sign up. Write "Delete my Prayer Lock account". We will reply and delete the account.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email address, or Google or Apple sign in ID).
  • Everything saved on your phone by the app: prayers, feelings, streak, badges, locked apps list and settings.

What may be kept

  • A small daily counter used to limit how many prayers one account can ask for (the account ID and a number per day). It holds no prayers and no personal details. Email us if you want it removed too.
  • Payment records. These are kept by Google Play or the Apple App Store, not by us. Deleting your account does not cancel a plan. Cancel your plan in your store account settings first.
