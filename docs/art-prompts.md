# Art prompts for ChatGPT (image generation)

Already designed on `Prayer Lock App Branding Showcase.png`, so NOT in these prompts:
app icon, logo, splash screen, sign up screen, featured image, praying frames, reading-Bible frames,
celebrate frames, and the small cross, heart, star, church, Bible and cloud icons.

5 sheets remain (Sheet 5 is the animation frames). Sheets are merged as far as quality allows. A 1536 px wide picture cannot hold more than
about 40 small icons or 12 big pictures without going soft. If ChatGPT gives you a larger size (2048 or more),
you can ask for the same sheets again with more cells.

How to use:
1. New ChatGPT chat. Attach `Prayer Lock App Branding Showcase.png` as the style reference.
2. Paste the Style block. Then paste ONE sheet prompt. Ask for the largest size and "as sharp as possible".
3. Save each result as PNG and send me the paths. I cut the cells, remove the white background and use them.

---

## Style block (paste first)

```
You are the art director of "Prayer Lock", a Christian prayer app that locks distracting apps until you pray.
Use the attached image as the exact style reference and keep it identical in everything:
- Cute warm 2D cartoon, thick dark-brown outlines on icons, smooth soft shading, gentle glow and sparkles.
- Colours: warm peach and orange, soft pink and lavender clouds, golden yellow for the cross, lock and hoodie,
  deep charcoal for text. Friendly, hopeful, calm. Christian but gentle.
- The same little girl: wavy dark-brown hair, rosy cheeks, yellow hoodie, purple-blue trousers.
- Logo lock: golden padlock with a white cross and glowing rays.
Output a SPRITE SHEET: a strict grid, equal cells, one item per cell, plain white background (#FFFFFF),
items centred and the same scale, nothing touching or crossing a cell edge, NO text unless I ask.
Do not redraw the items already on the attached sheet. Wait for my next message.
```
